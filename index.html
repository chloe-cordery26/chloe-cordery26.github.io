Research programme

Three levels of Human-AI work

01

AI-assisted knowledge work

Investigating how generative AI changes scientific evidence search and synthesis, including performance, information-seeking, source engagement, attention and neural activity through behavioural measures, eye tracking and fNIRS.

02

Human-AI collaboration & feedback

Investigating how collaborating with AI versus another person changes individuals’ relationship with their work and responses to performance feedback, integrating facial EMG, behavioural and self-report measures to examine feedback implementation and future performance.

03

AI & organisations

Examining how AI reshapes roles, expertise, trust, psychological safety, adoption and collective capability within organisations, connecting individual Human-AI interaction with organisational change.

Approach

Human-centred, multimodal research.

I combine process-level measures with task outcomes to understand not only whether AI changes performance, but how it changes the cognition and behaviour underlying that performance.
